10. IMPORTANT — HOW TO DETERMINE THE PRICING BAND

Because we collect both device count and cloud-user count, please use the higher of the two quantities to determine the customer's pricing band.

Example:

8 devices + 8 cloud users → 1–10 → $500/month

18 devices + 15 cloud users → 11–20 → $750/month

22 devices + 28 cloud users → 21–30 → $1,000/month

30 devices + 25 cloud users → 21–30 → $1,000/month

If either number exceeds the currently supported automatic pricing range, route the customer to the Custom Cybersecurity Plan flow until I provide the additional pricing bands.





11. INTERNAL COST TRACKING

Separately from the customer-facing price, the backend should be capable of tracking our underlying service cost using:

Endpoint: $2.00 per protected device/month

Cloud user: $2.50 per protected cloud user/month

Internal calculation:

(Device Count × $2.00) + (Cloud User Count × $2.50)

Example:

30 devices × $2.00 = $60

25 cloud users × $2.50 = $62.50

Internal cost = $122.50/month
